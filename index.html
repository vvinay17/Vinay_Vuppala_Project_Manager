About

Business thinking, run like engineering

My path runs Business Specialist → Business Analyst / Product Owner → Technical Project Manager & Product Owner, across RealPage, Mastercard, and Bank of America. That means I don't just manage timelines — I understand the data behind the decisions, the backlog behind the roadmap, and the stakeholders behind the sign-off.

A Master's in Business Analytics from the University of North Texas keeps my approach grounded in evidence rather than guesswork. Day to day, that's end-to-end Agile/Scrum & SAFe delivery, cross-functional stakeholder management across engineering, compliance, and business teams in regulated fintech and banking environments, and a data analytics lens on prioritization and credit risk.

Before any of that, I worked directly with the founders of Crave International to understand their business requirements and target users — my first lesson in owning a program end-to-end. I've also stayed close to the entrepreneurship and public-service side of business, including strategy work with a state-run innovation initiative and a central-government-affiliated MSME chapter — more on that below.
