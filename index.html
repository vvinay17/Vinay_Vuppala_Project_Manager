About

Business thinking, delivered like engineering

My path runs Business Analyst → Business Specialist → Business Analyst / Product Owner → Technical Project Manager, across RealPage, Mastercard, and Bank of America. Each move added a layer: first reading the data, then owning the backlog, now running the whole delivery.

A Master's in Business Analytics from the University of North Texas sits underneath all of it — I default to evidence over opinion, whether that's a predictive model or a sprint retro. In practice that means stakeholder management, requirements analysis, backlog prioritization, and risk management aren't separate skills to me — they're one continuous discipline of turning ambiguity into a shipped outcome.

Before any of that, I founded Crave International, an events and startup-incubation venture in Hyderabad — my first real lesson in owning a program end-to-end, negotiating with vendors, and keeping a cross-functional team pointed at one deadline.
